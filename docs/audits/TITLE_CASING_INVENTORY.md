## Counting and classification

An occurrence is a source-defined display slot or finite controlled wording variant, not a count of DOM nodes in a particular visit. Independent pages, navigation surfaces, reports, operation cards and conditional literal variants remain separate. Repeated data rows and shared renderers are counted once only where their consumers are explicitly identified in the occurrence note. Consequently totals measure the UI vocabulary and source occurrences, not the number of visible headings at once. Dynamic arbitrary-title slots and their stable interface fallback branches are separate evidence rows. Category tabs and source-selector tabs count; ordinary actions do not.

Title = linguistic Title Case, including proper nouns, initialisms and lowercase minor words. Sentence = sentence case with proper names preserved. Mixed = hybrid capitals without an established linguistic convention. Other = deliberate uppercase, branded/stylised text or arbitrary dynamic titles. A single ordinary capitalised word is compatible with both Title and Sentence; assigned **Title** consistently for counting, without claiming it demonstrates a convention. Known proper feature name Classics and brand name Book Club are preserved; “Australian classification” is Sentence. Controlled templates with numbers/provider names are classified from their fixed words; arbitrary film, cycle, user and private-set titles remain Other. Representative dynamic outputs below are synthetic source evaluations, never live/private data.

Validation: all 252 occurrence rows have unique IDs and existing source-file/line anchors; the screen and role breakdowns reconcile to the four category totals. Git status confirmed that this report is the only added/changed path.

The inventory includes semantic H1–H4, Empty/Failure titles, NativeDialog headings, disclosure/group labels, prominent overlines, statistic-card descriptors, navigation and deliberately styled film-title slots. Body/error paragraphs, ordinary form labels/buttons, chart axes/legends, score value labels, normal member rows and data lists are excluded unless they label a distinct group. HTML title is recorded separately below, outside the in-app heading total.

## Significant inconsistencies

- Home equivalent section headings mix `Last turn` / `Current turn` with `Quick Facts`, `Club Timeline` and `Classics Snapshot`. Statistic-card descriptors similarly mix `Days Active`, `Cycles Completed`, `Watch Time` with `Films brought`, `Already seen by all`, `Missing answers`. `Next Classics` and `Eligible Classics` are title-compatible but also sentence-compatible when Classics is treated as the feature name; they are not strong inconsistency evidence by themselves.
- Metrics report headings mix `Top Directors`, `Ratings Profile`, `Popularity & Obscurity` with `Genre fingerprint`, `Theme fingerprint`, `Talent fingerprint`, `Studio fingerprint`, `Release decades`, `Genre detail` and `Median reported budget / revenue`. General's `Top 5 Revenue / Budget Ratio` contrasts with adjacent sentence-case reports.
- The same popularity/obscurity roles use `Most popular` / `Most obscure` in Top / Bottom and `Most Popular` / `Most Obscure` in Cabinet. Cabinet's six `Most recurring Director` (etc.) headings use a lowercase modifier followed by a capitalised common occupation: Mixed rather than a proper-name exception.
- Admin operation headings use Title Case (`Populate Missing Scores`, `Fill Missing TMDB Metadata and Artwork`) beside sentence-case `Admin · swap current turn` and disclosures. Dialog titles mix `Add Classic` with `Score abbreviations`, `Remove from Classics` and `Remove event from History`; `Use from Builder` is compatible with both conventions because Builder is a named destination.
- Film Detail uses `Seen It` as a two-word column title beside `Haven't`, and sentence-case `Classics score`, `Crunchy math`, `Book Club appearances`. Capitalisation of Book Club itself is a proper-name exception, not Mixed.

No casing policy has been selected and no automatic transformation is proposed.

## Justified exceptions and exclusions

- Authenticated Home brand `Book Club`, sign-in `BookClub`, and all 18 deliberately stylised non-Home anagrams are Other. Uppercase taglines, Seen overlines and onboarding welcome are deliberate typography/copy patterns.
- IMDb, TMDB, LB, MC-U, MC, RT-A, RT-C, CLSC and uppercase identity comparisons are not evidence of inconsistent casing. Provider/proper names remain intact in otherwise classifiable headings. Trakt and Ebert one-word selector labels are Title-compatible, not proof of a policy.
- Film titles are listed only at deliberate title/heading render sites and classified Other. Private-set/cycle titles and uppercase identity profile group headings are likewise data-driven exceptions. Ordinary member names, provider-generated theme/studio/country labels, years, scores and chart data are not converted into interface-heading findings.
- Ordinary buttons including `All sets`, `Save set`, `View Watch Order`, `Add to Set`, `Record an Event`, Retry, Logout and navigation-styled action shortcuts are excluded as actions. Destination tabs and the Account Admin link are included. Plot More/Less controls are body disclosures, not section titles. AU watch-offer categories are metadata labels, not headings.
- Inline validation, auth configuration errors, maintenance run feedback, Seen save errors and stale-data banners are body/status paragraphs without a separate deliberate heading. Failure's actual H2 and Empty's H3 are included. LoadingView's text is a loading status beside skeletons, not a heading. Private-set deletion confirmation is a prose question with actions and no visible title, so it adds no heading occurrence.
